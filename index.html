What TrendMesh is

TrendMesh is a private, single-operator tool. A content creator uses it to plan short-form videos and to track how their own videos perform across TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels, for example as part of content-reward campaigns.

For TikTok, the tool connects the operator's own account through TikTok Login Kit and reads public statistics of the operator's own videos (views, likes, comments, shares) through the TikTok Display API. It does not access other people's accounts and does not share or sell data.

TrendMesh can also upload videos the operator produced and individually approved to the operator's own accounts. Every upload is confirmed by the operator beforehand; nothing is published automatically without that confirmation.

Who uses it

Only the operator. TrendMesh is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.
